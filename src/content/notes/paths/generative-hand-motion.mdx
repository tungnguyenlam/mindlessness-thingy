The other paths end at a language model that predicts the next token. This one ends at models that predict a whole clip of 3D hand motion at once. They reuse the same [[transformer-block]], but train it to undo noise instead of to continue text.

<Timeline path="generative-hand-motion" caption="The two bimanual hand-motion pipelines on this path and the ideas each adds. Their stages and numbers come from their model data (hover for the source)." />

The first three steps are shared with the language-model paths: the block, [[mha]] and [[rope]]. Here attention isn't causal, so every frame can see the whole clip and there is no cache to keep.

The next three are the generative part. [[flow-matching]] says what the network learns: the velocity that carries noise to data. [[adaln]] says how a condition such as the flow time enters every block, through the scale and shift after each norm. [[dit]] puts the two together into a denoiser.

[[mano]] is the output format: pose and shape parameters per hand that a fixed skinning model turns into a 3D mesh. [[stablehand]] combines these pieces to recover two-handed motion from noisy video tracks using a learned trust score per wrist and finger. [[flowhoi]] uses the same 8-block `AXD` DiT skeleton to synthesize coupled bimanual hand and 6D object trajectories from a first RGB frame, a language prompt, and a 3D Gaussian Splatting scene in two stages.

If you have read a language-model path, start at [[flow-matching]].
