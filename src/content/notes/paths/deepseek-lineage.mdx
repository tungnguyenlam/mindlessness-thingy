DeepSeek's models are easiest to understand as a chain of fixes. Two pressures drive almost every step: the [[kv-cache]] limits how long and how many conversations a model can serve, and dense feed-forward layers make every extra parameter cost compute on every token.

<Timeline path="deepseek-lineage" />

The first half of the path follows attention. [[mha]] sets the baseline, [[mqa]] and [[gqa]] shrink its cache by sharing heads, and [[mla]] shrinks it further by caching a compressed latent instead.

The second half follows the feed-forward layer. [[moe]] lets parameter count grow without growing per-token compute, [[deepseek-moe]] refines how experts are cut up, and [[aux-loss-free-balancing]] keeps them evenly used without hurting the main training objective. [[deepseek-v2]] combines the two halves, and [[deepseek-v3]] scales it up with [[mtp]] and [[fp8-training]].

The last stretch returns to attention, now for million-token contexts. [[deepseek-v3-2]] adds learned sparse attention ([[dsa]]), [[deepseek-v4]] rebuilds attention around compressed sparse attention and widens the residual with [[mhc]], and [[deepseek-v4-1-flash]] adds hashed n-gram memory ([[engram]]) beside a much smaller KV cache.

Notes marked with a dashed outline are still placeholders. The path order stays the same as they get written.
