Qwen's open-weight flagships are easiest to read as a chain of mixer choices. [[qwen2-5-72b]] is dense [[gqa]]: every layer writes a [[kv-cache]]. [[qwen3-235b-a22b]] keeps that attention and grows parameters with [[moe]] instead of width. [[qwen3-5-397b-a17b]] then spends most layers on [[gated-deltanet]], so the cache grows in only every fourth layer, and [[qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b]] scales that hybrid to a Max-class 2.4T model. [[qwen3-6-27b]] is the dense sibling on the same 3-then-1 layout.
