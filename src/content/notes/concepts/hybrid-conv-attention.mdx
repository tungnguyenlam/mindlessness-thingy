A full-attention stack pays for a [[kv-cache]] in every layer. Liquid's LFM2 search, run against on-device latency and peak memory, kept a simpler hybrid: most mixers are [[gated-short-conv]] blocks, plus a small number of [[gqa]] layers, and no extra state-space or linear-attention operator <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="§2.1" />. [[hybrid-ssm-attention]] answers the same cache problem by swapping in Mamba-2 instead of the convolution.

## The layout

Each layer is a pre-norm residual block: a mixer, then a SwiGLU feed-forward. The mixer is either the gated convolution or grouped-query attention with RoPE and QK-Norm <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="§2.2" />.

The released dense LFM2 sizes put 6 attention blocks in the 16-layer models and 8 in the 30-layer 2.6B <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="Table 1" />. LFM2.5 keeps that split. The 350M and 1.2B configs both list 10 `conv` layers and 6 `full_attention` layers, interleaved rather than grouped at one end of the stack <Cite id="lfm2-5-350m-config" where="layer_types" />.

Attention at those layers uses 8 key/value heads in every dense LFM2 size, with 16 query heads at 350M-class width and 32 at 1.2B/2.6B width <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="Table 1" />.

The convolution line does not move with context length. At 32K tokens the 350M-class KV cache is already hundreds of megabytes; the conv windows stay tens of kilobytes. That is the on-device point of the design: long prompts stay affordable because only a handful of layers grow.

LFM2.5 cards still quote a 32,768-token context for the 350M and 1.2B models even though `max_position_embeddings` is 128000 <Cite id="lfm2-5-350m-card" where="Context length" />. Treat the card figure as the supported length.

## How it compares

[[mla]] keeps attention in every layer and shrinks what each token stores. [[hybrid-ssm-attention]] replaces most attention with a recurrent SSM. This hybrid replaces most attention with a kernel-3 convolution and keeps a few full GQA layers as the global path. LFM2's hardware-in-the-loop search found that adding SSM or linear-attention branches on top of this mix did not improve the quality–latency–memory frontier they measured on device <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="§2.1" />.

Models built this way include [[lfm2-5-350m]], [[lfm2-5-230m]], [[lfm2-5-1-2b-instruct]] and [[lfm2-5-2-6b-base]].
