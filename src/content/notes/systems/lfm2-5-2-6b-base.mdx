LFM2.5-2.6B-Base is the pretrained 2.6B hybrid that [[lfm2-5-2-6b]] is post-trained from. It is still [[hybrid-conv-attention]], but deeper than the 16-layer 350M/1.2B models: 22 [[gated-short-conv]] blocks and 8 [[gqa]] blocks <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-base-card" where="Number of layers" />. Pretraining is about 34T tokens, the vocabulary is doubled to 128K, and mid-training extends context to 128K <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-blog" />. The card is for further fine-tuning, not chat out of the box <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-base-card" />.

`layer_types` places the 8 attention layers at indices 2, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 24, 27 — every two or three convolutions, not a block of attention at the end <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-base-config" where="layer_types" />. Feed-forward width 10752 matches LFM2-2.6B in the original report <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="Table 1" />.

<Callout type="pitfall">
The card lists context 131,072. This Base config has `max_position_embeddings: 128000`; the post-trained [[lfm2-5-2-6b]] config has 131072. Prefer the card for the supported window, and do not assume the two configs agree.
</Callout>

## What is new

### Deeper stack, bigger vocab, 128K context

The 16-layer models stop at 6 attention blocks and a 65,536-token vocabulary. Base adds two attention blocks and twelve convolution blocks, trains on 34T tokens, and doubles the tokenizer to 128K by extending the existing vocabulary in place rather than retraining from scratch — the same procedure used for LFM2.5-8B-A1B <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-blog" />. Mid-training includes a dedicated 128K context-extension phase <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-blog" />. [[rope]] θ is $10^7$ here versus $10^6$ on the 1.2B configs <Cite id="lfm2-5-2-6b-base-config" where="rope_parameters.rope_theta" />.

Original LFM2-2.6B was a 32K-context dense model <Cite id="lfm2-report" where="Table 1, §2.2" />. The 2.5 Base checkpoint is that depth and width with the longer window and the larger vocabulary.

## What it reuses

- [[hybrid-conv-attention]] with kernel-3 [[gated-short-conv]] and [[gqa]] (32 query / 8 KV heads, same as the 1.2B).
- SwiGLU feed-forward at the LFM2-2.6B width 10752.
- [[kv-cache]] only in the 8 attention layers.
