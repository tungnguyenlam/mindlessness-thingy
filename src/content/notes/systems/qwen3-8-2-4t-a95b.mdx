Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B is the first Qwen-Max-class model released as open weights. The mixer recipe is [[qwen3-5-397b-a17b]]'s: three [[gated-deltanet]] layers then one gated [[gqa]] layer, each with [[moe]], repeated 23 times <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-card" where="Hidden Layout" />. What changed is the width, the expert hidden size, and that this checkpoint is text-only with thinking required.

## The whole model

Ninety-two layers, hidden size 8,192, 512 experts with 10 routed plus one shared, expert width 2,048 <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-card" />.

The operator is unchanged: kernel-4 [[gated-deltanet]], gated [[gqa]] with QK-Norm, softmax top-k [[moe]] plus a sigmoid-gated shared expert, one [[mtp]] block <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-config" /> <Cite id="qwen3-5-moe-modeling" />. Partial [[rope]] still rotates 64 of 256 head dims ($\theta = 10^7$), but there is no `mrope_section` because this config has no vision tower <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-config" where="rope_parameters" />.

Every response from these weights starts with a think span; the card says thinking cannot be turned off and multimodal inputs are not supported <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-card" where="API Usage" />. Native context is 262,144 tokens, extensible to 1,010,000, matching Qwen3.5 <Cite id="qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-card" where="Context Length" />. Training-token counts for this generation are not on the card or in the config, so they are omitted.

## What it reuses

- [[gated-deltanet]] in 69 of 92 mixers.
- [[gqa]] with an output gate in the other 23.
- [[moe]] with 10-of-512 routed experts plus one shared expert.
- The [[qwen3-5-tokenizer]] 248K vocabulary (vision specials unused).
