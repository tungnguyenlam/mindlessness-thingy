Nemotron 3 Nano is NVIDIA's smallest Nemotron 3: a hybrid Mamba-attention MoE of 31.6B parameters, 3.2B active per token <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="§1, §2.1" />. The rest of the family grows from it — [[nemotron-3-super]]'s report says it "scales up the hybrid Mamba-Attention MoE architecture introduced in Nemotron-3 Nano" <Cite id="nemotron-3-super-report" where="§2.1" /> — and [[nemotron-lineage]] lays out the whole line. Nano predates the family's two defining additions: it has neither [[latent-moe]] nor [[mtp]] <Cite id="nemotron-3-super-report" where="Abstract, §1" />.

The layout is the [[hybrid-ssm-attention]] recipe that Nemotron-H introduced, replacing most attention layers with Mamba ones <Cite id="nemotron-h-paper" where="Abstract" />, fixed here as a single pattern <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="Fig. 2" />. The pattern, with M for Mamba-2, E for MoE and \* for attention:

```
MEMEM*EMEMEM*EMEMEM*EMEMEM*EMEMEM*EMEMEMEM*EMEMEMEME
```

<Callout type="note">
Table 1 counts two shared experts. The released config lists one at twice a routed expert's width, and the model card says "128 experts plus 1 shared" — both readings unverified here, so treat the count as unsettled.

## What is new

### Fine-grained experts beside a shared one

The dense feed-forward layers of the Nemotron-H hybrid are replaced by sparse MoE: each token is routed to a few of many experts, with shared experts that every token also passes through <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="§2.1" />. Six of 128 experts <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="Table 1" /> keep the active compute small while the parameter count grows. The mechanism is [[deepseek-moe]]'s; [[moe]] explains routing and gating.

### None of the family's later additions

No [[latent-moe]], no [[mtp]]. Nemotron 3 Super is called the first in the family with both <Cite id="nemotron-3-super-report" where="Abstract, §1" />, so Nano's experts work at full width and its training predicts one token per step.

## What it reuses

- **The hybrid stack.** Mostly [[mamba-2]] layers, a few [[gqa]] attention layers as the only [[kv-cache]] holders — the layout [[hybrid-ssm-attention]] explains.
- **Plain plumbing.** RMSNorm, no biases or dropout, untied embeddings, no positional embeddings <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="§2.1" />.
- **Squared-ReLU experts.** The experts activate with a squared ReLU and a sigmoid-gated router picks them <Cite id="nemotron-3-nano-report" where="§2.1" />.
