Inkling 2 Mini is a 26.4B-parameter multimodal [[moe|Mixture-of-Experts]] [[looped-transformer]] designed to fit in ~20 GB for local devices while matching larger models on agentic coding <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-blog" where="Introduction" />. Its key additions are **cross-pass attention-residual messages** between loops, **per-visit KV caches**, and native [[nvfp4]]/MXFP8 training and deployment <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-blog" where="Architecture and training" />.

## Cross-pass messages across four blocks

The decoder has 24 physical layers arranged in four 6-layer blocks: a prelude (layers 0–5), two middle blocks (layers 6–17), and a final block (layers 18–23) <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-blog" where="Looping design" />. The middle 12 layers run three times, yielding 48 layer visits per token from 24 stored layers <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-card" where="§2 Architecture type" />.

Rather than feeding pass $k$'s output blindly into pass $k+1$, every pass starts from the prelude output and reads a "message" from the previous pass's residual blocks via a learned gate <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-blog" where="Looping design" />. That gate starts closed during training so each pass initially behaves like a standard model, then opens gradually for stable optimization.

## Attention, MoE, and specifications

Each 6-layer block mixes five sliding-window layers (1,024-token window) and one full-attention layer with 24 query heads and 8 KV heads <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-card" where="§2 Architecture type" />. Each visit routes tokens to 8 of 128 experts; each extra pass adds ~1.0B effective active parameters (3.4B unique active, 5.4B effective active), and all 48 visits keep their own KV and short-convolution state <Cite id="inkling-2-mini-card" where="§2 Parameters" />.
