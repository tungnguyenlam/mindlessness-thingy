LFM2.5-230M is a compressed [[lfm2-5-350m]]. It keeps the [[hybrid-conv-attention]] recipe — [[gated-short-conv]] plus [[gqa]] — but drops two convolution layers, narrows the feed-forward, and is distilled from the 350M rather than trained as a from-scratch sibling <Cite id="lfm2-5-230m-blog" />. This note is those cuts. The mixers are unchanged; follow the links.

The 14-layer pattern in `layer_types` is two convolutions, then attention and convolution alternating, ending on a convolution <Cite id="lfm2-5-230m-config" where="layer_types" />. Attention layer count stays 6, the same as the 350M, so the [[kv-cache]] still grows with six GQA blocks.

<Callout type="pitfall">
`max_position_embeddings` is 128000 in the config; the card's supported context is 32,768. Same extra-room mismatch as the 350M.
</Callout>

## What is new

### Distill, then a short post-train

The 230M is not a from-scratch 14-layer LFM2. The blog's recipe is three stages after 19T tokens of pretraining (including a 32K context-extension phase): supervised fine-tuning with distillation from LFM2.5-350M, direct preference optimization, then multi-domain reinforcement learning <Cite id="lfm2-5-230m-blog" />.

Relative to the teacher, the visible shrink is width in the feed-forward (2560 vs 6656) and two fewer convolution layers, with hidden size and head counts unchanged <Cite id="lfm2-5-230m-config" where="intermediate_size" />. The card still points the model at extraction and lightweight tool use, and away from heavy math, code, or creative writing <Cite id="lfm2-5-230m-card" />.

## What it reuses

- The [[hybrid-conv-attention]] stack from [[lfm2-5-350m]], with the same 16/8 [[gqa]] heads and kernel-3 [[gated-short-conv]].
- Vocabulary 65,536 and 32,768-token context, matching the other small LFM2.5 text models.
